How a Reg CF raise works

From first conversation to closing, most raises take four to six months.

  1. Weeks 1–4

    Test the waters

    Gauge interest publicly before filing anything, and collect non-binding reservations.

  2. Weeks 2–8

    Prepare

    Pick a platform, get financial statements reviewed or audited, set your terms.

  3. Weeks 6–10

    File Form C

    Your offering statement goes to the SEC on EDGAR and live on your platform.

  4. 21+ days

    Run the campaign

    Investors commit through the portal. They can cancel up to 48 hours before close.

  5. Ongoing

    Close and report

    Funds release once you hit your target. File an annual report each year after.

The rules on one page

Current as of November 2026. The SEC is discussing raising the cap to $20M, but nothing has changed yet.

Maximum raise$5,000,000 per company in any 12 months
Who can investAnyone 18+, accredited or not
Investor limitsNon-accredited: $2,500 to $124,000 a year across all Reg CF deals, based on income and net worth. Accredited: no limit.
Where it happensOnly on an SEC-registered funding portal or broker-dealer
DisclosureForm C filed with the SEC, plus a Form C-AR annual report afterward
ResaleShares generally can't be resold for one year
